Post-Secondary Pre-Tertiary Venture

Dual Enrollment STEAAM Curriculum

Allowing talented deserving female scholars to take university-level courses that transfer directly as equivalent college credits to world-class institutions.

The 2-Year Bridge

College Credit Transfer & Degree Acceleration

College credit for aligned degrees and career choices is a crucial target at the onset. OLA collaborates with the Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) School of Computer Science and College of Fine Arts, expanding to CMU-Africa College of Engineering and leading HBCU partners.

Upon completing their 2-year residency at OLA, students transfer their college-equivalent credits to accelerate completing their bachelor’s degrees abroad, reducing university duration and financial burden.

Accreditation & Quality Assurance Enlisted the Edmund Gordon Institute for Urban and Minority Education (IUME) at Columbia University Teachers College to enrich, monitor, and evaluate course delivery standards.
