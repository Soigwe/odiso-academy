100% Free Education Equity

Zero-Cost Admission for
Nigeria’s 36 States

There are no application, tuition, or boarding fees at OLA. Talented girls from each state are selected based on financial status through Nigeria's Social Register (NSR).

Admission Principles

Dedicated to the Poorest of Poor Talented Girls

Only 0.7% of the 4.4M young females aged 14–24 in Nigeria have tertiary education. OLA was established as a not-for-profit school to systematically shatter this barrier.

100% Free Tuition: All academic, dual-credit courses, and laboratory materials fully funded.
Full Residential Boarding: Housing, meals, health, and laptops provided at Lagos Free Zone.
National Quota: Scholars drawn from all 36 states and the FCT based on National Social Register (NSR) data.
