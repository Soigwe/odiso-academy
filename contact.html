Girls from each of Nigeria’s 36 states are selected based on financial status, targeting deserving girls documented in Nigeria’s Social Register (NSR). OLA is the sole not-for-profit non-parochial education equity school equipping this vulnerable demographic to excel in STEAAM.
OLA is collaborating with Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) School of Computer Science & College of Fine Arts, CMU-Africa, and leading HBCUs to offer courses that transfer as equivalent college credits. The program is monitored and evaluated by the Edmund Gordon Institute for Urban and Minority Education (IUME) at Columbia University Teachers College.
Beneficiaries sign a binding agreement to return to Nigeria after completing their bachelor’s degrees abroad to serve in the OLA STEAAM Teachers Corps (fulfilling their NYSC obligation) and undertake graduate studies in STEAAM to mentor future cohorts.
Our only current campus location is at Lagos Free Zone, Ibeju-Lekki, Lagos State, Nigeria, where the 2026 pilot of 15 resident students will be housed and taught.
